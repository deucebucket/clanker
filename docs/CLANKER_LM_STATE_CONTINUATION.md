## Semantic and release boundaries

Positive simple state continuation only. Negated STILL, compound operator
scope, perfect intervals, REMAIN predicates and absolute-duration questions
remain incomplete. Fronted operators are restricted to single top-level state
clauses, not questions or arbitrary embedded/compound discourse.

Fronted AGAIN preserves unresolved temporal-versus-speech-act repetition;
it cannot affirm temporal recurrence solely from that form. Fronted STILL
carries concession without inventing a contrast proposition. Neither creates
a prior event. Historical/modal/reported/conditional/quoted evidence cannot
rewrite unrelated current direct appraisals.

After unsupported new state wording, an old supported appraisal can still be
recalled. A broader uncertainty/blocking policy for incomplete updates remains
needed. This is recorded as a next failure, not counted as solved.

Keep draft pending parent integration, #123 candidate-runtime evaluation
binding, exact-head full CI and independent review. No old corpus, benchmark
identity, checksum or integrity assertion was altered. Fresh sessions use the
new component contract; old operator-less snapshots are not auto-migrated.

How Do You Read the Room?

A 30-sentence emotional baseline assessment

You will see 30 sentences describing situations and feelings. Some are clearly negative. Some are clearly positive. Some are ambiguous.


Rate each one based on how it makes you feel when you read it. Not what you think it means objectively -- how it lands in your gut.


There are no wrong answers. This measures your emotional range -- how deep you go, how high you reach, and where your neutral sits.
